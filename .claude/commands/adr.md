Load the `writing-an-adr` skill. Ask me the decision in one sentence if I have
not already said it, then:

1. Copy `docs/adr/0000-template.md` to the next free number.
2. Fill in the context, the decision and the alternatives from what we discussed

3. Write the consequences section honestly — including what this makes harder,
   and what would make the decision wrong later.
4. Tell me the row to add to the table in `architecture.md` and to chapter 29 of
   the Figma page.
