## The problem

What we were trying to do, and why the obvious thing did not work. Two or three
sentences. Someone reading this in a year has no memory of the week it was
written.

## What we decided

One sentence, in the present tense: "Photographs are processed by a worker, not
in the request."

## Why

The reason this option won. Name the constraint it respects that the others
broke — cost, a cold start, a free tier limit, something a couple would notice.

## What we did not do

The options that lost, one line each, with the reason. This section is the point
of the whole document: it stops the same argument being had twice.

## What this costs us

Every decision costs something. Say what. If the answer is "nothing", the
decision was not a decision.

## How we would undo it

The rollback, in one or two lines. If there isn't one, say that instead.
